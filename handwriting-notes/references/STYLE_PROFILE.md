# Handwriting reference analysis

Examine 3–7 photographs from the same consenting author. Record:
- Cyrillic letter variants and repeated-character variability.
- Letter joins, pressure, thickness, ink hue and occasional pen lifts.
- Word spacing, line spacing, drift and variable slope.
- Typical headings, numbering, underlining, bullets and arrows.
- Page margins, paper tone, orientation and desk lighting.

Choose the clearest academic note for layout, additional notes for ordinary variations. Do not confuse artificial distortion with authentic handwriting. Do not include users' personal writing samples in public releases.
