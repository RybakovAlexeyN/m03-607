At the edge of the sea stands a green oak;
a golden chain hangs upon that tree.
By day and by night a learned cat
walks around it, following the chain.
To the right he starts to sing a song;
to the left he tells a fairy tale.
Wonders live there: a woodland spirit wanders,
and a mermaid sits among the branches.
Along mysterious, unknown paths
lie the tracks of creatures nobody has seen.
A hut on chicken legs stands there
without a window or a door.
Woods and valleys are filled with visions.
At dawn waves roll onto a deserted sandy shore,
and thirty splendid knights emerge one after another
from the shining waters, with their uncle from the sea.
